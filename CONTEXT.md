# Performance Management

This context defines the language used throughout the annual KPI and staff-appraisal workflow.

## Language

**Annual KPI Review Period**:
A configured performance cycle with its own dates, deadlines, allocations, review frequencies, and configuration snapshots.
_Avoid_: Appraisal year, assessment year

**Review Checkpoint**:
The end of a Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual performance interval for which Self and Superior assessments are completed.
_Avoid_: Review Period, assessment period

**Superior**:
The employee assigned to approve Individual KPIs and review KPI and Attitude assessments for another employee.
_Avoid_: Reporting Manager, reviewer, manager when describing this relationship

**Point**:
A raw assessment rating from 1 to 5.
_Avoid_: Score when referring to the raw rating

**KPI Score**:
The weighted result obtained from an Annual KPI Point and the KPI's weightage.

**KPI Performance Score**:
The sum of an employee's weighted KPI Scores for an Annual KPI Review Period.

**Attitude Evaluation Score**:
The normalised result calculated from the Superior's Points for every active criterion applicable to the employee's format.

**Final Appraisal Score**:
The configured weighted combination of KPI Performance Score and Attitude Evaluation Score.

**Ready for Appraisal**:
A derived indicator showing that all required KPI checkpoints and the Attitude Evaluation are Reviewed.
_Avoid_: Treating it as an appraisal lifecycle status

**Overdue**:
An indicator that an activity remains incomplete after its deadline or was completed late.
_Avoid_: Treating it as a lifecycle status

**Requirement Gap**:
A business rule awaiting stakeholder confirmation that the prototype must expose without inventing behaviour.
_Avoid_: Assumption, default rule

**Active-Period Workflow Data**:
Records created through the current prototype workflow and reused by every downstream page, calculation, dashboard, and export.
_Avoid_: Page mock, copied active-period data

**Historical Demo Data**:
Read-only sample results for earlier Annual KPI Review Periods, used only to demonstrate trends and prior appraisal history.
_Avoid_: Mixing historical samples into the active-period workflow

**Attitude Configuration Snapshot**:
The Published Attitude Evaluation configuration preserved when an Annual KPI Review Period opens and used throughout that period.

**Demo Preparation**:
A prototype-only simulation action that completes remaining prerequisite records with deterministic values while preserving work already completed manually.
_Avoid_: Production shortcut, relaxed readiness rule
